## Rollback and delivery notes

- Apply migration `0011_group_conversations.sql` before deploying group-aware chat/realtime services, then deploy the frontend. Once group data exists, rollback to a pre-group application is **not supported**; use a forward fix or restore a pre-feature backup. Do not delete group data as an automatic rollback step.
- Group-call signaling remains ephemeral Redis-coordinated Pub/Sub. Active foreground participants reconcile missed terminal events through `group.call.sync` every 10 seconds; this does not guarantee convergence while disconnected or during sustained network failure.
- The conversation outbox consumer is at-least-once: if publish succeeds but acknowledgement fails, duplicate projections may be delivered. Consumers/UI apply projections idempotently by membership revision.
